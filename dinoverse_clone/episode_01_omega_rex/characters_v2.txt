-- CREATURES (one sheet per creature — lock BEFORE generating its zone) --
CARNO=Carnotaurus: bull-horned theropod, thick brow horns, knobby red-brown pebbled hide, blunt boxy snout, comically small vestigial arms.
QUETZ=Quetzalcoatlus: giraffe-tall azhdarchid pterosaur, very long toothless beak, short white-grey fuzz (pycnofibers), folded 10-meter wings when grounded.
DUNK=Dunkleosteus: 6-meter armored placoderm fish, bony plated head shield, self-sharpening BONE SHEAR-BLADES instead of teeth, dark grey body fading to an eel-like rear.
MOSA=Mosasaurus: bus-sized marine reptile, crocodile-like jaws, paddle flippers, downturned tail fluke, grey-green counter-shaded hide.
RAPTOR=Utahraptor: FEATHERED, man-sized dromaeosaur — dark slate plumage with rust barring, feathered arm-wings, long stiff tail fan, huge sickle toe-claw. NEVER scaly or naked (the S46 myth-bust depends on the feathers).
STYRACO=Styracosaurus: rhino-sized ceratopsian, one long nose horn plus a frill ringed with long spikes, mottled olive hide.
BRACHIO=Brachiosaurus: crane-necked sauropod, front legs longer than hind, small high-domed head, weathered grey-brown hide.
ARGENTINO=Argentinosaurus: the biggest animal in the park — hugely massive-bodied titanosaur, pillar legs, long tapering neck and tail, pale sand-grey hide. Visibly bulkier than the Brachiosaurus.
TREX=Tyrannosaurus rex: massive deep-skulled apex theropod, battle-scarred green-brown hide, tiny two-fingered arms.
INDOMINUS=Indominus: pale bone-white hybrid theropod, quill-like osteoderms down neck and back, unusually long four-clawed arms, red eyes.
DREX=D-Rex: the deepest-kept hybrid, clearly DISTINCT from the Indominus — larger, charcoal-black hide with faint glowing orange seams, oversized asymmetric jaws, six-clawed hands, armored ridged back, unsettling wrong proportions.

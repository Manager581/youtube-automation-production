## What DIVERGES (the rewrite targets)
1. **Narration carrier.** Flip from **Dee-VO-led → banter-led.** His enclosures run on host+companion dialogue; ours run on Dee monologue + NARRATOR. Biggest "feel" gap.
2. **On-camera STAFF.** His ranger speaks diegetically (safety + a fact) and is *in frame* at scale — a realism anchor. Ours: 2 staff lines, 1 staff still in 95 shots. → stage a ranger in select enclosure stills.
3. **Cold open.** His is a **45s, 30-cuts/min montage hook** that teases the payoff. Ours is a **20-shot Dee-VO intro** — long and monologue-y. → rebuild as a fast banter+tease hook.
4. **Mosasaurus** — he has no dedicated Mosasaurus scene (just a throwaway line); we added a full 6-shot one. Fine to keep, but it's our addition, not from his template.
5. **Incident** — his is compact (72s, banter + PA/alarm). Ours sprawls (6 Hybrid + 11 Breakout + 5 Outro = 22 shots, panic-heavy). → tighten.

# ChatGPT Render Kit — Sudbury Operations — Dry Facility

How to use: for each shot below, start a fresh ChatGPT message, **attach the
listed images** (they're in `pipeline/inputs/`), paste the prompt, and send.
Ask for landscape/wide format if it comes back square. If a detail is off,
reply with a correction ("make the coveralls brighter orange", "the vending
machine goes at the hallway entrance") — iterating in the same chat keeps
the layout consistent.

## overview — Isometric dollhouse overview

**Attach:** `floor-plan.png`, `chatgpt-attempt.png`, `coveralls-interior.png`

```
Create a photorealistic 3D isometric cutaway 'dollhouse' rendering of this mine dry facility, viewed from above at a 3/4 angle, matching the attached floor plan EXACTLY — same room positions, same hallways, same fixtures. The second attached image is a rough previous attempt: keep its camera style but greatly improve realism, lighting and color. The third attached photo shows the real orange coveralls to replicate on the racks.

Mine dry / change facility at a Sudbury mine site. Proposal concept visuals for a site-services RFP response by Source Atlantic.
Approximately 48 ft x 42 ft interior, single storey, concrete floor.
Layout:
- Main entrance at the south wall: black-framed glass double doors with dark safety mats just inside.
- Immediately left (west wall) when walking in: a long clothing rack hung with orange hi-vis coveralls with reflective striping.
- Mid-room, west side: a row with a boot-wash grate pad, orange boot cubbies (2 rows x 6 columns), and a 2-ft-deep storage block.
- Center of the open room: five parallel drying rails hung with rows of orange coveralls.
- A small wooden bench in the open area.
- The north-west portion of the room is open to the rest of the building (no wall, labeled 'open to building').
- Right (east) side: two parallel hallways, each 7 ft wide. The upper hallway (yellow-coded) has a vending machine at its entrance and serves a bathroom at its north end, plus a change room and bathroom on its east side. The lower hallway (pink-coded) serves a change room and bathroom on its east side and a bathroom at its south end.
- Change rooms have wooden benches; bathrooms have a toilet and sink each.
Proposed innovations to include:
- TV Innovation Hub: A freestanding navy media wall with a large wall-mounted TV screen near the building-side entry of the dry, with two stools; displays live safety metrics, shift notices, and continuous-improvement ideas.
- Micro-Market Vending: Two modern glass-front smart-vending micro-market units in a dedicated zone near the main entry, separate from the hallway vending, stocked with PPE consumables and quick supplies.

Render at high quality: soft studio lighting, realistic shadows, Clean professional architectural visualization for a client proposal. Realistic materials: painted concrete block walls in warm grey, sealed concrete floor, safety-orange coveralls, hi-vis yellow/silver reflective striping. Bright even industrial lighting. No people, no text overlays, no watermarks.
```

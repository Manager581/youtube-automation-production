Finally! NASA Responds To Claims About Betelgeuse

For years, astronomers have been watching Betelgeuse with growing concern. This red supergiant star, one of the brightest in our night sky, has been acting strange. The star began dimming dramatically in 2019, sparking fears that it might be about to explode. A supernova this close to Earth would be visible in broad daylight. It would outshine the full moon. Scientists around the world turned their telescopes toward this massive star. NASA's satellites monitored its every change. The question everyone was asking: is Betelgeuse about to die? And what would that mean for Earth? After years of speculation, NASA has finally released their official response. What they discovered will change everything we thought we knew about this giant star.

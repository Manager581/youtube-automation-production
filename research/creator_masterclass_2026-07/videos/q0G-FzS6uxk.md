# "Every mistake preventing you from getting views on YouTube" — MonomoTime (q0G-FzS6uxk, 10:56, 128K views)

## What the video is
Algorithm-advice listicle from a small creator (self-described: "finally got a channel monetized... nothing crazy at all"). Seven failure modes: account trust, AI-slop crackdown, originality vs "too original", fake engagement/self-promo, niche selection, CTR packaging, hooks/retention.

- Direct quote from a YouTube CEO blog post on "AI slop": building on anti-spam systems to reduce "low-quality repetitive content" (~2:32).
- Direct quote of the community guidelines originality standard: original/authentic, significantly transformed if borrowed, not mass-produced or repetitive (~4:04).
- A Roblox channel example ("rare anomalies within Roblox") as niche-within-niche (~7:39).
- Everything else is asserted, uncited: "shadow ban", bot classification of new accounts, "several big channels demonetized despite human effort", "YouTube relies on Gemini to understand the entire context of a video", "too original gets punished — this has been proven."

## Credibility
Medium-low motive, medium signal. No course; only Amazon affiliate links for mic gear unrelated to the advice. He's honest about modest results, which raises trust but lowers authority. The strongest material is the verbatim YouTube policy quotes (verifiable). The "trust score" / "shadow ban" framing is folk theory — YouTube publishes no such score — but the underlying behaviors (new-account throttling, verification unlocking distribution features) are plausible and free to act on. The Gemini-detection and big-channel-demonetization claims are unverified but directionally consistent with the July-2025 inauthentic-content policy this operation already tracks as risk #1.

## Tactics extracted
1. **Account warm-up before first upload** (NEW): aged account, or months of real watching/liking/commenting; enable the third feature-eligibility setting (phone/ID verification) in Studio — claims it raises trust even on aged accounts. Free.
2. **72-hour indexing window** (NEW, evidenced): expect zero views/impressions for ~3 days on a new channel while YouTube classifies the video. Don't iterate packaging before day 3.
3. **AI-slop policy intel** (CONFIRM + NEW detail): ChatGPT-script + AI-VO "doesn't work anymore"; partial human effort not always enough; Gemini allegedly reads whole-video context. AI as refinement/brainstorm tool stays fine.
4. **"Too original" penalty** (NEW framing, CONFIRM in practice): packaging (title/thumbnail/concept) must be adjacent to existing channels so YouTube knows which audience to push to; put originality inside the video, not the framing. This is exactly the viral_recreation_spec / measured edit-grammar approach.
5. **No low-intent self-promo** (NEW rule): Reddit/social sharing brings clickers who don't care → tanks retention now and home-feed CTR later. Net loss unless the audience is precisely targeted.
6. **Niche-within-niche** (CONFIRM): maps to topic scorer fresh_perspective/blind_spot.
7. **Title/thumbnail-first ideation; scrap vague ideas** (CONFIRM): maps to title_thumbnail + five_second_title tests.
8. **Hook = longest-worked script section; must pay off the thumbnail** (CONFIRM): maps to intros.json 0-15s micro-window and the deep-watch practice.

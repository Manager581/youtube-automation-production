2. **Niche criteria**: <5 small channels with views>subs; no 100K+ incumbent; no small channels failing; niche <6 months old; monetizable; long-form bonus — CONFIRM of how Rexcaped's niche was chosen (views>subs outliers).

5. **Search-bar supply check**: search the exact topic; if done 3+ times with no new angle, drop it — hardcore fans saw it already. ADOPT as a mechanical evidence input to the 9-test scorer (originality/fresh_perspective).

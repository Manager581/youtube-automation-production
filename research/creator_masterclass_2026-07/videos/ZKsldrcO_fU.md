# The New Rules of YouTube (2026) — vidIQ (ZKsldrcO_fU, 9:38, 63.8K views)

## What the video is
Algorithm-advice / motivational piece from vidIQ's main channel (~2.5M subs). Thesis: subscribers no longer drive views — YouTube is a per-video recommendation engine (browse/suggested matching one video to one viewer), so any small channel is "one upload away." Second half is a strategic claim: in an AI-flooded feed, the defensible asset is identity/brand, and "lazy, say-nothing faceless channels" get "squeezed into oblivion."

## What's actually shown (evidence)
- One case study (~0:31): The Spencer Howard Show, 66 → 2,000+ subs off a single 100K+ view video. n=1, survivorship-selected, but a real checkable channel.
- A pointer to YT Studio → Analytics → Audience tab → subscriber watch-time panel as self-serve proof subscribers barely matter (~0:31).
- Host's own history: ~300 videos / 5 years before "figuring it out" (~3:04); vidIQ still ships underperformers at 2.5M subs (~1:33).
- No revenue screenshots, no dashboards, no policy citations. Everything else is asserted.

## Credibility
vidIQ sells its SaaS (top description link vidiq.com/start); the video is top-of-funnel brand content, not a course pitch — soft-sell, no affiliate links. Claims are directionally consistent with known YT mechanics (browse/suggested dominance, per-video evaluation). The one mechanical rule (24–48h CTR+AVD vs channel average) is standard practice, credible. The "faceless channels get squeezed" claim is an opinion/prediction, not evidence — but vidIQ is industry-adjacent enough that it's a meaningful leading indicator of platform sentiment.

## Tactics extracted
1. **Per-video evaluation; subscribers ≈ irrelevant for reach** — CONFIRM. This is the premise of launching Rexcaped/Prehistoric POV from zero. Evidence: Spencer Howard case + Audience-tab check.
2. **24–48h post-upload decision rule** (~5:07): if CTR AND AVD beat the channel's own averages → "good video" → do more of the same: direct sequel, double down, playlist, promote that type. — NEW as a formalized post-upload step; the playbook covers pre-production (topic scorer, edit grammar, thumbnails) but has no codified upload-feedback loop.
3. **Jab-before-hook** (~4:05): lead with value/entertainment, zero asks; CTA-heavy videos repel. — CONFIRM (intros.json 0–15s micro-window, retention_delivery).
4. **Volume = data, not algorithm juice** (~5:38–6:40): post a lot because every upload is feedback; breakthrough can be video 10/50/100; make the next 12 months an "upload year." — CONFIRM of the frequent-uploads thesis (bizdoc channel rationale), and a prod for Prehistoric POV (1 video, stalled).
5. **Barbell** (~7:10–8:41): AI tools on one end + "radically, authentically human" identity on the other; undifferentiated faceless AI content in the middle dies. — CONTRADICT (partial) of the operation's faceless model; the operative word is "say-nothing." Defense = format identity + recognizable voice, which the op can build without a face.

# Wholesale Ted — "Why Getting Sales & Traffic Is Now On 'Easy Mode'" (9iP588aMFBc, 13:13)

## What the video is
Algorithm-advice / funnel video from Sarah (Wholesale Ted, e-commerce niche). Thesis: modern platforms (YouTube, Etsy, TikTok, Amazon...) replaced popularity-bias ranking with multi-armed-bandit "test impression" recommenders, so a brand-new account with zero following can get free traffic if content passes small cohort tests. Three tips: (1) be persistent — every upload triggers a fresh test; (2) pick low-competition niches (uses Claude for gap research); (3) package titles/descriptions so an AI algorithm can classify the audience.

## Credibility
Motivated reasoning: the video is self-sponsored (her free ebook → $1/7-day Econ Clubhouse trial funnel) plus Shopify/Printify/.store affiliate links. The "easy mode" frame lowers the perceived barrier for buying her course. The test-channel screenshots are unverifiable (channel unnamed) and survivorship-prone. That said, the mechanistic description of test impressions/bandit exploration is broadly consistent with how these recommenders publicly work, and the retro-testing-of-back-catalog anecdote matches many creators' reports. Treat the mechanics as plausible, the "every video has a fair chance" claim as oversold.

## Tactics extracted
1. **Never delete/unlist early flops** — after one video passes a test, the algorithm re-tests old videos on the new audience cluster (her monetization came from a resurrected flop). NEW as an explicit rule → upload practice.
2. **Judge a channel after ~5 uploads, not 1-2** — each upload is an independent cohort test; persistence until one passer classifies the channel. CONFIRM (cadence already assumed) with a NEW concrete threshold.
3. **Niche-down for algorithm classifiability** — the more specific the content, the easier the cohort pick. CONFIRM → topic scorer / channel identity.
4. **"AI-legible" packaging** — write title/description so a machine can unambiguously infer the target audience, not just for human CTR. Half-NEW angle → titles_thumbnails module.
5. **LLM gap research for low-competition niches** ("identify neglected customers"). CONFIRM — cruder than the existing 9-test scorer.
6. Notion-AI vibe-coded Etsy products — irrelevant to this operation.

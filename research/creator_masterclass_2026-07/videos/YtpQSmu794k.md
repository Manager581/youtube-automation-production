# Shane Hummus — "$333,080.07 From One YouTube Channel Using Claude AI" (YtpQSmu794k, 36:32)

## What the video is
A workflow tutorial doubling as a coaching funnel. Shane Hummus (careers/degrees channel, claims 1.5M subs) walks his 8-step Claude-driven system: idea → title → thumbnail → intro → script → pre-production → record → post-production → upload. He records the videos; Claude "skills" + a team do everything else (~1-1.5h of his time/video, 4h/week max).

## What's actually shown (evidence)
- [00:00-03:06] YouTube Studio AdSense screen: $333,080.07/year, live page refresh, settings shown as USD, then deliberately flipped to Taiwan/Jamaican dollars to expose the fake-guru currency trick.
- [01:03] A faceless video on his own channel: 1.4M views, $7,800, ~1,000 views/day after 3+ years.
- [12:45-13:45] Claims his top title ("9 Boring But High-Paying Online Jobs...", $62K AdSense) was Claude-written, picked from 5-10 generated options.
- Client results ($185K/mo Josh, $80K/mo Nicole, $500K/mo Sean, "Whiz of Ecom" 7 figures) — asserted, zero evidence shown.

## Credibility
Above genre average but still a funnel. The revenue demo (refresh + USD check) is more verifiable than most, and the anti-slop stance matches YouTube's real "inauthentic content" policy. But: the $333K comes from a mature 1.5M-sub high-RPM channel, not a new faceless one; "4 hours/week" hides an undisclosed team cost; every CTA routes to a free-training → coaching funnel ("Harvard of YouTube coaching", <18% acceptance, 3-5 spots) plus a "Claude Bundle" lead magnet of deliberately simplified skills. Client numbers are unverifiable. Advice content itself is sane and mostly generic.

## Tactics extracted
1. **Icon method** — paste 3-4 niche channels into Claude, find videos massively outperforming each channel's average; outliers = next videos. With own data, rank by revenue not views. → maps to topic scorer (candidate-sourcing feed) / ideation.json. Partial CONFIRM (Rexcaped already clones measured winners), NEW as an explicit multiplier-vs-channel-average feed for bizdoc.
2. **Holy trifecta** — generate title + thumbnail concept + intro in ONE pass; congruence beats quality ("7/10 but congruent"); intro's first seconds must visually look like the thumbnail. → intros.json + titles_thumbnails.json. Congruence-as-a-gate is the NEW bit.
3. **Anti-fragile scripting** — need a 10-item list, generate 15, cut 5. "Every video is a listicle." NEW micro-tactic → scripting.json.
4. **YAP session** — voice-dictate (Wispr Flow) stream-of-consciousness context to Claude, then have it interview you, second yap, then draft; train script skill on own transcripts. CONFIRM-ish (Claude-Code-driven scripting exists).
5. **Editor brief** — Claude writes on-screen text, cut points, emphasis per line. CONFIRM — our paper-edit JSON is the machine version of this.
6. **Retention pass** — drop timestamped transcript of the rough cut into Claude, ask where retention drops and how to fix; advanced variant frame-samples every ~3s (he skips it for turnaround). NEW cheap pass; we already have WhisperX + contact sheets.
7. **Upload package** — Claude writes SEO description/tags/chapters/pinned comment; tags low-value but 20s; pin comment + reply in first hour. CONFIRM (Rexcaped upload package) + NEW pinned-comment habit.
8. **Thumbnail tools** — Claude weak at image gen; ChatGPT best, Nano Banana second; Pikzels ("pixels") and 1of10.com for paid batch. CONFIRM our ChatGPT recipe; paid tools = ignore (hard rule).

## Policy notes
YouTube fine with AI as a tool; "inauthentic content" (100% AI, mass-produced, robot voice, zero effort) → demonetization or ban. **Bans can propagate to every channel on the same AdSense account.** AI avatar presenters (HeyGen + voice clone) → uncanny valley + demonetization/shadowban risk. He recommends real voice even when faceless (cartoon-character client, 7 figures, real voice).

## What we apply
- **All 3 channels**: verify AdSense/account linkage — one strike must not be able to kill all three. Pin a comment at publish + reply first hour.
- **Rexcaped**: overage scripting on list beats (gen 15, keep 10); congruence gate (first 5s must match thumbnail subject) added to upload checklist; retention-prediction pass on video 2's transcript before render lock.
- **Prehistoric POV**: icon-method outlier scan of 3-4 POV channels to pick video 2's topic before scoring.
- **Bizdoc**: outlier feed (ColdFusion/HMW/etc. view-multipliers) upstream of the 9-test scorer; generate 3-5 cold-open variants and pick; RPM-first selection = CONFIRM of channel thesis.

## Verdict
Moderate value. No new capability, but 3-4 free micro-tactics worth adopting, one sharp policy detail (AdSense linkage), and confirmation that the operation's human-directed pipeline sits on the right side of the slop line. Ignore the funnel, paid thumbnail tools, and the show-your-face push.

# "I copied Hormozi's YouTube Strategy and it made me $100K" — Mike and Matty (Eb8wlFawjTw, 813s, 26.9K views)

## What the video is
A B2B creator-marketing case study: run TWO channels as one funnel. Big "front-end" channel = reach/social proof; small "back-end" channel = conversion content for people who already like you. Claims their 20K-view back-end video "generated over $100K" of attributed business revenue vs $13K AdSense from a 7.7M-view front-end video. Everything is oriented at business owners selling a service — not AdSense channels.

## What's actually shown (evidence)
- Claims only, narrated over B-roll: $13K from 7.7M views (~$1.7 RPM, plausible for their niche), "$100K+" from a 20K-view video (attributed coaching revenue, unverifiable from transcript). "84% show rate" for prospects who watched videos — self-reported, small-sample, admitted ~few months of sales experience.
- Hormozi mechanics cited: second channel ("Mermozie" per transcript) launched Sept 2025, first video **collab-posted** with the main channel; near-daily volume; low-production, vague-titled, CTA-dense clips positioned as client interactions.
- Self-demonstrating: the description's 3 links each carry `?video=Eb8wlFawjTw` — per-video attribution parameters. The video itself is the funnel it teaches.

## Credibility
Motive is explicit: they sell one-on-one founder coaching ("Work With Us"), a "Niche Navigator," and a "0 to $1M Creator Playbook." The dollar figures are marketing, not receipts. BUT the mechanics are internally consistent and practiced on-screen (CTAs, attribution params, end-screen handoff), which makes the *mechanical* claims more trustworthy than the revenue claims. The intro tease that "YouTube will launch one of their most powerful features… for business owners" is never substantiated — hype.

## Tactics extracted
1. **Two-channel front-end/back-end funnel** — NEW but inapplicable: we have no service offer or sales calls. Maps to nothing; IGNORE.
2. **Collab-post a video across channels to seed the smaller one** — NEW platform-feature tactic. Directly usable: Rexcaped ↔ Prehistoric POV share a dino audience, and the Omega Rex variant exists on both.
3. **Per-video attribution params on every description link** (`?video=<id>`) — NEW, free, mechanical; visible in their own description. For bizdoc sponsor links (Incogni/DeleteMe/etc.), this makes revenue-per-video computable.
4. **Engineered end-screen handoff**: verbally pitch ONE specific video as "going way deeper" on the same topic; end-reachers are the highest-intent segment — CONFIRM + refine of playbook `retention_delivery` and Rexcaped's existing `sh18_cta_next` card (which is currently generic).
5. **Measure each channel by the metric matching its role** (revenue-per-view, not views) — CONFIRM of the bizdoc high-RPM thesis; nothing to change.
6. **Back-end content style** (daily volume, low production, vague titles, wall-to-wall CTAs) — CONTRADICTS `titles_thumbnails` playbook, but only valid for a conversion channel we don't run. IGNORE.
7. **"Trust recession" — AI slop is eroding feed trust; evidence of realness converts** — CONFIRM of the layered-composite rationale (real footage + composited creatures) and the no-AI-faces rule.
8. **Homepage flooding via multi-channel volume for fans** — soft CONFIRM of the existing cross-channel variant practice (Omega Rex on Prehistoric POV + Rexcaped variant).

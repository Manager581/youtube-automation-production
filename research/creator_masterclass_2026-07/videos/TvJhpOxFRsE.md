# The New Way to Do YouTube Automation in 2026 — Make Money Matt (TvJhpOxFRsE, 22:19, 6.4K views)

## What the video is
A course-funnel pitch dressed as strategy: the "SMART system" (Strategic niche, Monetization-first, AI automation, Revenue diversification, Targeted audience). Thesis: stop chasing viral entertainment views; pick a "problem-rich" sub-niche (money/health/relationships/travel/tech), monetize day one via affiliate/course funnels, and use AI to cut per-video cost from $190 to ~$10. Everything conveniently terminates at his products: Tube Magic (scripts), vid.ai (stock-footage video gen), Tube Accelerator (program + "book a call"), plus a paid Emergent sponsor segment (affiliate link `via=makemoneymatt`).

## What's actually shown (evidence)
Almost nothing. No analytics, no revenue screenshots, no channel reveals. Claims ($10M earned, billions of views, "1,000-view channels out-earning 100,000-view channels") are asserted only. The single live demo is the sponsored Emergent app build (~13:16–15:49). Named reference channels are checkable but unverified in-video: Yellow Dude, Cruise Like a Pro, Brainy Dose, Passport Pro, The Purring Journal, Stoic Journal, Zennie Studio.

## Credibility
Low-to-moderate. Four stacked financial motives (sponsor, own course, two own SaaS tools with lifetime recurring affiliate commissions — he even recruits affiliates mid-video). The business-model logic is internally coherent and the cost math is plausible, but every recommendation funnels to something he sells, and the "views are vanity" frame is exactly what a low-view course seller would argue. Treat as directionally informative, evidentially empty.

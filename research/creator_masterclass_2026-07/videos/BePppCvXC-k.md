# WOLF Money — "I Proved $1,000/Day On YouTube Isn't Luck (Secret Channel Exposed)" (BePppCvXC-k, 626s, 682K views)

## What the video is
A day-by-day case study: he starts a brand-new Roblox gaming channel ("99 Nights in the Forest"), applies the MrBeast-style "take an outlier video, put 100x more effort in" method in a niche full of low-effort daily uploads, and reaches a claimed $1,000/day (CAD) by video 5. Narrative arc: 11 impressions for weeks → dead video explodes on day 14 → monetization sub-gate scramble → 3.5M-view viral sequel.

## What's actually shown (evidence)
- Screen-recorded YouTube Studio analytics throughout: 11 impressions after 2 days (videos 1-2); video 3 at 10 impressions after 5 days, 0 browse/suggested by day 10, then 1.5K views/day from day 14 [01:33-04:07].
- Impression sources: his Roblox video recommended off MrBeast Minecraft / Grow a Garden / Blox Fruits videos — wrong audiences while YT "figures out" the channel [03:05-03:36].
- Monetization sequence: watch hours banked but stuck at 999 subs; uploads a dedicated sub-CTA Short; analytics lag ~5 days; YPP acceptance → "monetize all" existing videos, but ~40K pre-acceptance watch hours earn $0 [04:37-06:40].
- Revenue tab reacted "live": $60/20K views (~$3 USD RPM), currency-switch to CAD; video 5 (34-min mythbusting sequel, 3 weeks of work) hits 600K views in 2 days, ~$6 RPM, $965/day channel peak, $21K CAD total, $24K/90 days [06:40-10:15].

## Credibility
The channel is never named despite "Secret Channel Exposed" — nothing independently verifiable. Description motive: vidIQ affiliate ($1 trial) crediting vidIQ + Airrack for the method, yet the video never mentions vidIQ once — the shown method is pure outlier-cloning + effort. Headline overclaims: n=1, and he admits at 00:00 he "actually got a little lucky." That said, the cold-start pattern (dead ~2 weeks, then explosion), the $3-6 gaming RPM, and the monetization-lag mechanics all match known platform behavior — the ops intel is credible even if the revenue is unauditable.

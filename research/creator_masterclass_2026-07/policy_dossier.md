## 2. The July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy — what practitioners report actually triggers it

### 2a. The confirmed baseline
- The **July 15, 2025 YPP update** renamed "repetitious content" to **"inauthentic content"**, targeting **mass-produced / repetitive** content. Referenced across essentially every analysis as the operation's #1 platform risk (8Mx2m0djgTA, QfbGKfkGP8U, eF75ZffgsUk, h6DB0e96GI0, lDpOnE3VJVI, NlfmQpSaYMo, KxsasFMMPpA, and others). **[OFFICIAL — policy exists; the per-video citations of it are analyst framing]**
- One video quotes the actual texts verbatim: a **YouTube CEO blog post** on reducing "low-quality repetitive content" building on existing anti-spam systems, and the **community-guidelines originality standard** — monetized content must be original/authentic, **significantly transformed if borrowed**, and **not mass-produced or repetitive** (q0G-FzS6uxk). **[OFFICIAL — verbatim quotes]**
- The **altered/synthetic-content ("Is it AI?") disclosure question** appears in the upload/stream publish flow — visible in the Studio UI (eF75ZffgsUk). **[OFFICIAL — UI feature]**

### 2b. What practitioners say actually triggers enforcement
- **"Mass-produced" is the rejection word YouTube itself uses at YPP review** — and the decisive case used a **human voice**: a subscriber's channel was rejected as "mass-produced" because all videos were stylistically identical; the remedy that worked was *delete lookalike videos, change style, reapply* → accepted (4xWQ_fHLGAc). Corollary from the same source: **AI voiceover per se is not the trigger** — his channels all use AI VO and passed YPP. **[PRACTITIONER]**
- **Detection is template-sensitive.** A politics-template farmer warns "try not to work on the same template" and admits active community-guideline trouble (0SPqPnpQsWE). A $35K/mo Shorts operator **rotates his caption template every ~10 videos** specifically because "identical templates could get demonetized" (PtO7jd8L2xs). Two independent operators converging on the same evasion = the detection is real and reads the **edit fingerprint across uploads**, not just within one video. **[PRACTITIONER — corroborated x2]**
- **Channels are being declined at YPP review in 2025–26, and appeals with a good case succeed**: a student channel was declined, then monetized after an appeal (ipbnR92Elxg). **[PRACTITIONER]** → Direct action for us: archive the human-editorial paper trail per video as an appeal packet (§6).
- **Banned AI channels share spam-pattern similarities, not AI usage** — "find ideas yourself, QC scripts yourself, keep human input, deliver real value" is the claimed safe recipe (LGYiPA5SKvw); "enforcement targets AI-slop usage patterns, not AI tools" (tICnK3Qb2k8). **[PRACTITIONER — consistent with the official policy text, no specifics]**
- **Fabricated "facts" are their own enforcement lane**: invented content = "deceiving the audience" → demonetization or removal (zJmYdcvwY1Y). **[PRACTITIONER — consistent with misinformation policy]**
- **Sudden-stop heuristic**: a young channel with hit videos that abruptly stops uploading ≈ YPP denial/removal for that format ("90% probability" is his number, not policy). Useful *field signal* for niche vetting — scan 3–5 comparable channels before entering a format (zJmYdcvwY1Y). **[FOLKLORE — but a free, observable pre-check we adopted into the topic-scorer workflow]**
- **"YouTube uses Gemini to read whole-video context and suppress low-effort AI pre-algorithm"** (q0G-FzS6uxk). **[FOLKLORE — uncited; directionally consistent; logged as unverified]**
- **"Trust score" / account warming / shadow bans** (8KXlxAKOTdE, q0G-FzS6uxk, 54UTQ2kFhuA "premonetized accounts bypass trust score"). No such published system exists. **[FOLKLORE]** The only free derivative worth keeping: phone/ID feature-eligibility verification before first upload, and a 72-hour no-evaluation window on new-channel uploads (q0G-FzS6uxk — the 72h claim is backed by one real analytics screenshot). **[PRACTITIONER-weak]**
- **Loop-streaming a pre-recorded video as 24/7 "live" to farm the 4,000 watch hours** is taught as "100% organic and monetizable" (eF75ZffgsUk) — it is a textbook inauthentic-content + misleading-metadata target. The one true fact inside it: **live watch hours genuinely count toward YPP** while public. **[OFFICIAL for the hours rule; the farming method is a trap]**
- **Cross-platform corroboration that repetitive-AI volume gets hard-capped**: TikTok Shop bans accounts near ~300 videos/30 days when content reads low-quality (r81ImbWaxEE) **[PRACTITIONER]**; Meta ran its own 2025 unoriginal-content crackdown, and a Facebook operator advises leaving Meta's "Add AI label" OFF — the negative example (E_CIP98ufto). **[PRACTITIONER]**
- **Watermark concealment is an aggravating signal**: two tutorials teach hiding the Flow/Veo watermark by scale-and-shift or overlay (NlfmQpSaYMo, lDpOnE3VJVI). Veo output carries SynthID invisible watermarking that survives cropping, so concealment removes nothing and demonstrates intent to obscure AI provenance at manual review. **[Analyst assessment on top of PRACTITIONER instruction — never do this]**

### 2c. Synthesis
The enforcement profile assembled from 32 videos: **sameness across uploads (template/edit-fingerprint), mass-production cadence, no visible human editorial layer, undisclosed AI, concealed provenance, fabricated content**. Not triggers: AI voice alone, AI tooling alone, faceless format alone. Our differentiation (measured edit grammar, composited real footage, original scripts, frame-level QA, provenance ledger) is the defense — the gurus' evasions are the confession.

## 3. Reused-content enforcement and Content ID behavior

All from operators living inside the enforcement, i.e. **negative-example intel**:

- **Content ID reliably catches raw movie clips.** The $35K/mo Shorts cloner adds "small animations" specifically "to get away from Content ID," and states raw movie clips are unmonetizable without added webcam + text layers (PtO7jd8L2xs). **[PRACTITIONER]**
- **Reused-content review tracks source platform**: he sources clips from TikTok/Instagram rather than YouTube because YouTube-sourced clips carry "reused content risk" for demonetization (PtO7jd8L2xs). **[PRACTITIONER]** — i.e., YouTube's reused-content matching is strongest against its own corpus.
- **"Text overlay makes reused clips transformative and monetizable" is asserted with zero evidence** (QfbGKfkGP8U — the claim rests on a currency-toggle dashboard trick). Low-value overlays that restate what's visible are what YPP reviewers flag; the claim also conflates monetization policy with copyright exposure, which licensing never enters. **[FOLKLORE — dangerous]**
- **Delete-and-repost of dead videos to a fresh channel** can trip reused-content review (8KXlxAKOTdE — taught as a tactic; our analysis flags the risk). **[FOLKLORE tactic, real risk]**
- **Competitor-transcript rewriting as your script** is a reused-content risk on top of being below our 95+ script bar (zJmYdcvwY1Y). **[Analyst assessment]**
- **Structural takeaway** (PtO7jd8L2xs analysis): generating **original AI assets composited into real footage sidesteps the entire reused-content enforcement vector** this genre spends its effort evading. Our fair-use armor for bizdoc stays untouched: ≤7s/clip, ≤30s/source, mute original audio, narration + zoom/crop + text overlay as transformative layers, on-screen "First reported by" credits (per CLAUDE.md rules; independently validated by ipbnR92Elxg's "real footage beats AI slop" admission).

## 6. Compliance checklist for OUR three channels

Everything maps onto systems that already exist — this is an enforcement layer, not a rebuild.

### 6.1 All channels — standing rules
1. **AI disclosure every upload.** Rexcaped and Prehistoric POV tick **YES** on the altered/synthetic-content question (photoreal AI creatures in real footage); bizdoc documents a written stance on cloned-voice VO disclosure before first upload (eF75ZffgsUk, E_CIP98ufto). Add as a line in each upload package (NEXT_VIDEO.md pattern).
2. **Never conceal provenance.** Any generated clip carrying a provider watermark ships disclosed-and-watermarked or is not used; watermark check added to the frame-level QA pass (lDpOnE3VJVI, NlfmQpSaYMo).
3. **Archive a per-video appeal packet**: script version history, sourcing docs, `verify_render.py` JSON+HTML reports, approval-ledger entries, provenance stamps. Declined-then-appealed-then-monetized is a proven path (ipbnR92Elxg); the packet is the evidence of transformative human work.
4. **Inter-video edit-fingerprint variation.** Before each publish, diff `extract_motion_events.py` output (cut density, montage placement, music/SFX pattern) against the channel's previous upload so `rexcaped_edit_engine.py` / the FFmpeg renderer never stamps an identical template twice — the extension of the clip-variety rule from intra- to inter-video (0SPqPnpQsWE, PtO7jd8L2xs).
5. **Hard NOs codified** (§4): no aged/pre-monetized channel purchases, no multi-accounting or credit farming, no account farms or identical cross-account uploads, no fake engagement, no loop-stream farming, no delete-and-repost across channels, no gray-market credit resellers, no competitor voice cloning.
6. **Monetization-survivor scan** as a standing pre-check beside `topic_scorer.py`'s source_availability test: before committing to any new format, check 3–5 comparable channels for the hit-videos-then-sudden-stop pattern (zJmYdcvwY1Y).
7. **FTC affiliate disclosure boilerplate** in every description template that carries any affiliate link (LGYiPA5SKvw).
8. **No low-intent seeding** (Reddit/social blasts) of uploads; 72-hour no-evaluation window after upload on young channels; feature-eligibility (phone/ID) verification completed before first upload (q0G-FzS6uxk, eF75ZffgsUk).
9. **When YPP arrives**: set membership tier prices manually in Studio → Earn → Memberships before smart-pricing defaults auto-apply; note the 12-month per-tier pricing lock (CQWfqKGFoPM).

## 6. The outreach motion (sequences, not spam — starts week 4, post-clearance only)

All from personal LinkedIn (free) + personal Gmail. Tracked in the `warm100_targets` Sheet (NOT any HubSpot instance — D2). Volume is deliberately low: this is warm, named-relationship selling — his day job skill.

**Wave 1 (week 4): Inner 15–20** — people who'd take his call anyway.
- Touch 1: personal note (LinkedIn DM or email): 3 sentences — what he built ("I turned my automated documentary pipeline into a studio — here's a spec piece on Shopify I made with it: [link]"), why them specifically (the trigger), one ask: "worth 20 minutes?"
- Touch 2 (+5 business days, non-responders): a genuine value jab, no ask — a 5-bullet mini-teardown of one of THEIR existing videos, or a one-pager "3 documentary stories [Company] could tell" (the jab-before-hook principle, ZKsldrcO_fU, tactic_map 2.6).
- Touch 3 (+5 more days): breakup + referral ask ("who on your team owns video?").

**Wave 2 (weeks 5–8): Outer 40–60**, released 10–15/week, same 3-touch shape, always trigger-led ("congrats on the raise — the story you told in the announcement post is a mini-doc waiting to happen").

**Discovery call (20 min, his native motion):** diagnose their current video motion → show the demo → present the 3 tiers → close for a Tier-1 or Tier-2 scope on the call or a proposal within 48h. Proposal = 2 pages from a template (deliverable, gates, dates, price, terms). No spec work beyond the one demo; no discounts below $1,500.

## 12. Metrics + kill/scale criteria

**Tracked weekly in the Warm-100 sheet:** touches sent, reply %, discovery calls booked, proposals out, close rate, $ invoiced, $ collected, hours logged per video (must trend 20→13), channel-floor health (did Rexcaped/bizdoc items from `action_plan.md` still ship? Y/N).

## 13. Capacity math (the cannibalization ceiling)

Total side-operation cap: **10–15 h/wk = 43–65 h/month.** Channel floor (Rexcaped V2 → next video + bizdoc upload + PPOV cadence per `action_plan.md`): **~5–7 h/wk ≈ 22–30 h/month, non-negotiable.** Client-work ceiling: **~30–35 h/month.**

| Load | Hours/mo | Fits? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 × Tier 2/month (steady ~13h + ~4h sales/PM) | ~17h | Comfortable — the sustainable default |
| 2 × Tier 2/month | ~30h | The hard ceiling — zero slack for a day-job crunch or a channel spike |
| 1 × Tier 3/month | ~28–32h | At ceiling; book only with a 20-business-day runway |
| 3+ videos/month | 45h+ | **Cannibalizes everything** — refuse; waitlist the client to next month (scarcity also defends price) |

Practical rule: **sell max 2 slots/month, deliver a median of 1.** Revenue ceiling inside the cap ≈ $6K/month at Tier-2 pricing (analyst estimate) — this play is a margin business on existing infrastructure, not a growth flywheel; if demand ever sustains >2 slots/month, the correct move is raising prices, not hours.

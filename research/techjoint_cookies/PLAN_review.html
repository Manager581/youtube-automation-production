Recipe — the ratios that actually make crispy/gooey happen

Yields ~14 large cookies · bake 375°F / 190°C · 10–12 min. Ratios are standard bakery-style; the three rules are what most home recipes skip.

IngredientAmountWhy (crispy/gooey logic)
Unsalted butter, browned, cooled 10 min1 cup · 226 grule 1 — melted/browned = chewier, crisper edges + toffee
Dark brown sugar, packed1 cup · 200 gmoisture → soft chewy centre
Granulated sugar½ cup · 100 gspread + crisp edge
Egg1 largestructure
Extra egg yolk1rule 2 — richness → gooey centre
Vanilla extract2 tspflavour
All-purpose flour2¼ cups · 280 gbody
Baking soda1 tspspread + browning
Fine salt¾ tspbalance
Chocolate — ½ chopped dark bar + ½ semisweet chips1½ cups · 255 gchunks → puddles, chips hold shape
Flaky sea saltto finishcontrast, bakery look

Method (as spoken)

  1. Brown the butter on medium, swirling: foams → quiets → golden flecks + toffee smell. Into the bowl, cool 10 min.
  2. Whisk both sugars into the butter ~1 min — looks like caramel.
  3. Whisk in 1 egg + 1 extra yolk + vanilla until glossy and thick.
  4. Fold in flour, soda, salt — stop the second no dry flour shows.
  5. Fold in the chocolate.
  6. Scoop big (~3 Tbsp / 80 g). Chill 30 min minimum.
  7. 375°F, 10–12 min — pull when edges are golden and centres still look underdone.
  8. Tap the tray once, flaky salt, rest 5 min on the tray. Then pull apart.

## 2. Recipe (real, tested ratios — this is what makes crispy/gooey actually happen)

Yields ~14 large cookies. Bake **375°F / 190°C · 10–12 min**.

| Ingredient | Amount | Why it's here (crispy/gooey logic) |
|---|---|---|
| Unsalted butter | 1 cup / 226 g, **browned**, cooled 10 min | RULE 1 — melted/browned butter = chewier, crisper edges + toffee flavour |
| Dark brown sugar, packed | 1 cup / 200 g | moisture → soft chewy centre |
| Granulated sugar | ½ cup / 100 g | spread + crisp edge |
| Egg | 1 large | structure |
| **Extra egg yolk** | 1 | RULE 2 — fat/richness → gooey centre |
| Vanilla extract | 2 tsp | flavour |
| All-purpose flour | 2¼ cups / 280 g | body |
| Baking soda | 1 tsp | spread + browning |
| Fine salt | ¾ tsp | balance |
| Chocolate — ½ chopped dark bar + ½ semisweet chips | 1½ cups / 255 g total | chunks melt into puddles, chips hold shape |
| Flaky sea salt | to finish | contrast, "bakery" look |

**Method (as spoken in the VO):**
1. Brown the butter: medium heat, keep swirling → foams → quiets → golden-brown flecks + toffee smell. Pour into the mixing bowl, cool 10 min.
2. Whisk both sugars into the butter ~1 min until it looks like caramel.
3. Whisk in 1 egg + 1 extra yolk + vanilla until glossy and thick.
4. Fold in flour, baking soda, salt — stop the second no dry flour shows.
5. Fold in the chocolate.
6. Scoop big (~3 Tbsp / 80 g) balls. **Chill 30 min minimum** (RULE 3).
7. Bake 375°F, 10–12 min — pull when edges are golden and centres still look underdone.
8. Tap the tray once on the counter, flaky salt, rest 5 min on the tray. Then pull apart.

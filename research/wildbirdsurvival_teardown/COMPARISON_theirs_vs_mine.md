## Story arc — shape of the two
- **Theirs = a single rising-relief arc.** One problem (parasites), one turn (river), one big payoff
  (fish), a second cleaner stacked on top, then calm + philosophy. **No villain, no danger spike.**
  Its back third (3:42–6:00) is deliberately low-energy — pure soothing cleaning.
- **Mine = the same spine + a second act.** Identical parasite→turn→cleaning front half, then I add a
  **predator climax** their #1 doesn't have. Rationale: their back half sags; a wordless lioness burst
  injects the retention spike their calmest section lacks — and a predator climax is proven on this
  channel (their mongoose-vs-cobra did 855K on exactly that). It's a considered bet, not a 1:1 copy.

## Visuals — where they overlap and differ
- **Shared:** gritty macro ticks, buffalo in water/mud, red-billed oxpeckers, flat natural light,

## What this comparison tells me to change in my board (adopt their proven numbers)
1. **Lengthen the pre-turn pause to ~12s** (they use 14.9s and it's their strongest anticipation beat).
2. **Extend the hero hold to ~30s** (they hold 35s; mine at 22s under-uses the money shot).
3. Keep the predator climax — it's my deliberate upgrade to their sagging back third, and it's a
   format they've already won with elsewhere (mongoose vs cobra, 855K).

# Episode 01 — Storyboard & Shooting Plan
_Built to the verified spec (FORENSICS.md + gate_style_wbs.py). Hero animal prototyped & approved
in Grok (grit look). All clip audio stripped; one music bed + light diegetic ambience + VO._

## Step 0 — the click (locked)
- **Topic:** the red-billed oxpecker & the Cape buffalo — the hand-sized bird that both *cleans* the
  buffalo of parasites AND *guards* it from lions it can't see. Two animals, curiosity gap, two
  jeopardies (parasites, then a predator). Proven-winner subjects; not a 1:1 of their #1 (fish-cleaning).
- **Title (pick one):** "The Tiny Bird That Decides If This Buffalo Lives" · alt: "Why a 1,700-Pound
  Buffalo Can't Survive Without a Bird This Small"
- **Thumbnail:** extreme close-up of the buffalo's eye with an oxpecker right beside it + 2–3 words
  **"IT SAVES HIM"** or **"LIFE OR DEATH"** (yellow/white, thick black outline).

## The 6-beat outline
1. **Cold open (0:00–0:15):** one buffalo, right now, tormented by ticks and flies. Visceral macro.
2. **Escalation (0:15–1:30):** it's losing the fight; it has tried everything; the parasites are winning.
3. **The turn (~1:30):** the oxpeckers arrive.
4. **Payoff I — the cleaning (1:30–4:00):** the birds work the hide, ears, wounds. **Music peak here.**
5. **Deepen (4:00–6:00):** the bargain — what the birds get, life lived on a giant.
6. **Climax — the guard (6:00–7:50):** a lioness stalks; the buffalo is blind to her; the oxpeckers'
   alarm erupts (the wordless action burst); the buffalo wheels and escapes.
7. **Resolution (7:50–8:30):** calm returns, birds resettle, one-line theme, departing shot.

**Runtime ~8:30 (510s). VO budget ≤765 words (~700 used). Narration coverage ~52%.**

## Storyboard (scene by scene)
Legend — **Cut pace:** shots per scene & length. **Music:** one continuous bed, dynamic noted.
**SFX = diegetic ambience only, low in mix. NO whoosh/riser on any cut.**

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN · 0:00–0:15 · 4 shots (1.6/2.2/1.6/5s) — fast
- **On screen:** (1) OPEN ON the buffalo's face, flies crawling at its eyes. (2) **EXTREME MACRO:**
  a cluster of engorged ticks packed in the skin folds behind the ear. (3) the buffalo jerks its
  head, flies scatter. (4) wide-ish: the lone buffalo in flat grey morning light, tail flicking.
- **VO:** *"Right now, this Cape buffalo is losing a fight it cannot win — not against a predator,
  but against hundreds of tiny things feeding on its blood."*
- **Music:** low sub drone, barely there.
- **SFX:** heavy fly buzz, one wet tail-flick, a single deep buffalo huff.

### SCENE 2 — ESCALATION · 0:15–1:00 · 8 shots (~5s each) — settling
- **On screen:** macro of ticks at the ears, neck, the soft skin between the legs. The buffalo rubs
  its head on a dead tree; rolls a shoulder in the mud; shakes; nothing works. Hooves churn mud.
- **VO:** *"Ticks have buried themselves around its ears, its eyes, the soft folds of its skin. Each
  one drinks a little more of its blood. The buffalo does what it always does — it rubs against bark,
  rolls in the dust, shakes its whole body. And still, they hold on."*
- **Music:** the bed enters — slow, minor, patient strings, quiet.
- **SFX:** flies, bark-scrape, mud squelch, low grunt. *(No cut hits.)*

### SCENE 3 — THE TOLL · 1:00–1:30 · 6 shots (~4–5s) — settling
- **On screen:** the buffalo stands still, exhausted; a raw, weeping tick wound; eyes half-closed;
  flies at the wound. A slow push-in on the tired eye.
- **VO:** *"Left alone, the wounds turn raw. Infection follows. A grown buffalo can be worn down —
  not by one bite, but by thousands."* **[PAUSE 3s]**
- **Music:** holds, adds a low cello note under the pause.
- **SFX:** flies, faint labored breathing.

### SCENE 4 — THE TURN · 1:30–2:00 · 5 shots (2s wordless beat, then 4–6s) 
- **On screen:** **[MUSIC-ONLY 4s]** a flurry of wings enters frame → a single red-billed oxpecker
  lands on the buffalo's shoulder. Then two more. Sharp little eyes, coral-red bills.
- **VO:** *(after the wordless landing)* *"And then — help arrives, on wings."* **[PAUSE 2s]**
  *"The red-billed oxpecker. A bird small enough to sit in your palm."*
- **Music:** a lift — the first hopeful motif, still restrained.
- **SFX:** wingbeats, the oxpecker's rising *churrr* call. *(Diegetic, not a stinger.)*

### SCENE 5 — PAYOFF I: THE CLEANING · 2:00–3:00 · mix incl. a 22s HERO HOLD — the money shots
- **On screen:** the bird hops to the ear and **works a tick free** — macro. **HERO HOLD (~22s, near
  wordless):** an oxpecker scissoring ticks off the neck, one after another, the buffalo's eye
  softening. Then 3 quicker shots: birds along the spine, under the jaw, at the wound.
- **VO (sparse over the hold):** *"It works fast — snipping each parasite loose with a bill built for
  exactly this."* **[HOLD, music carries ~12s]** *"For the first time in days, the buffalo stands still."*
- **Music:** **PEAK of the whole video here** — the theme opens up, warm, full. (First third ✓)
- **SFX:** tiny bill-clicks, soft bird chatter, a long slow buffalo exhale.

### SCENE 6 — THE BARGAIN · 3:00–4:00 · 12 shots (~4s) — body pace
- **On screen:** more birds arrive; they comb the whole animal; one drinks at the rim of a wound; a
  bird tucks a tick away; the buffalo grazes calmly while covered in busy little passengers.
- **VO:** *"This is not charity. Every tick is a meal. The oxpecker feeds on the very parasites that
  were bleeding the buffalo dry — and on the blood at the edges of its wounds. Two animals, each
  taking exactly what it needs from the other."*
- **Music:** eases down from the peak into a steady, contented middle.
- **SFX:** bird chatter, grazing, distant savanna birds.

### SCENE 7 — LIFE ON A GIANT · 4:00–5:15 · 14 shots (~5s) — body pace, some holds
- **On screen:** the birds ride the moving buffalo; preen on its back; a pair courts between the
  horns; they bathe in the buffalo's shadow at a waterhole; sleep tucked against its warm flank at dusk.
- **VO:** *"The buffalo becomes their whole world. A moving island of food, warmth, and safety. They
  raise the next generation here — born, fed, and taught to fly from the back of a creature a thousand
  times their size."* **[PAUSE 4s over the dusk shot]**
- **Music:** softer, wandering, a touch of wonder.
- **SFX:** low wind, evening insects, settling wings.

### SCENE 8 — THE SECOND DANGER BEGINS · 5:15–6:15 · 10 shots (5–6s, tightening)
- **On screen:** golden-grey dawn. In the tall grass **behind** the grazing buffalo, a lioness's face
  parts the stalks — the buffalo doesn't see her. Cut buffalo (calm) / lioness (low, creeping) /
  oxpecker (head snapping up, alert). Tension building.
- **VO:** *"But the oxpecker does more than clean. It watches. From the buffalo's back, it sees what
  the buffalo cannot —"* **[PAUSE 2s]** *"— and this morning, something is moving in the grass."*
- **Music:** the warm bed curdles — a low tension drone, a single ticking pulse begins.
- **SFX:** grass rustle, the lioness's soft padded steps, sudden silence of the small birds.

### SCENE 9 — THE ALARM · 6:15–7:10 · **THE WORDLESS BURST — 10 sub-2s cuts** — climax
- **On screen (WORDLESS, ~40s, music+SFX only):** the oxpecker **erupts** — wings flare, a shrieking
  hiss. Rapid cuts: bird screaming / buffalo's head snapping up / lioness breaking cover / buffalo
  spinning to face her, mud flying / the herd wheeling in / lioness skidding, aborting the charge /
  dust. The oxpeckers scatter and re-land.
- **VO:** *none.* **[MUSIC + SFX ONLY — this is the payoff of the whole build]**
- **Music:** **peak intensity** — the tension pulse snaps into a driving hit-bed, then cuts to near-silence
  as the lioness aborts.
- **SFX:** the piercing oxpecker alarm hiss (the hero sound), thundering hooves, a lion snarl, wingbeats.

### SCENE 10 — RESOLUTION · 7:10–8:30 · 8 shots (holds, ~8–14s) — slowing
- **On screen:** the lioness melts back into the grass, hungry. The buffalo, breathing hard, calms.
  One by one the oxpeckers return to its back and resume picking, as if nothing happened. Soft dawn
  light. Final: the buffalo walks away into the mist, birds riding, tail swinging.
- **VO:** *"The lioness leaves hungry. The buffalo never even saw how close it came."* **[PAUSE 5s]**
  *"A giant, kept alive by a bird the size of a hand. Not out of kindness — but because, out here,
  the ones who survive are the ones who never stand alone."*
- **Music:** resolves — the warm theme returns, gentle, then fades under wind.
- **SFX:** calmer bird chatter, wind, one last soft huff. *(No CTA in the video; subscribe lives in the description.)*

## Production notes
- **Clips:** ~60 Grok i2v gens, seeded from ONE locked hero-buffalo still + one oxpecker still for
  identity consistency. Use the **grit prompt recipe** (flies, mud, flat/overcast light, telephoto,
  handheld, "no golden hour") — proven to match the look.
- **Audio:** strip every clip's audio (`-an`). Build 3 layers in the FFmpeg renderer: (1) one Pixabay
  music bed with the dynamic above, (2) a low diegetic ambience layer (flies/wind/bird calls/the alarm
  hiss), (3) the ElevenLabs VO on top. VO = deep male, high stability, ~150 wpm.
- **QA:** frame-strip every clip (physics/anatomy); run `gate_style_wbs.py` on the final render → 11/11.

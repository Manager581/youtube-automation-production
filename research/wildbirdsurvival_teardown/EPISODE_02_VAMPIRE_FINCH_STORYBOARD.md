## Step 0 — the click (locked)
- **Topic:** the vampire ground finch (*Geospiza septentrionalis*) & the Nazca booby, on Wolf/Darwin
  Islands, Galápagos. A seed-eating Darwin's finch that pierces a seabird twenty times its size and
  drinks its blood — and the giant *lets it happen*. Combines their three top-winning ingredients at
  once: **scale-gap** (654K lane), **gross/blood** (their gross lane), and **violation of expectation**
  (a *finch*, a seed-eater, drinks blood). Two animals, curiosity gap, two jeopardies (the bleeding,
  then the eggs). Not a 1:1 of their #1 fish-cleaning hit.
- **Title (pick one):** "Why Does This Giant Seabird Let a Finch Drink Its Blood?" · alt: "Everyone
  Thought This Finch Ate Seeds. It Drinks Blood."
- **Thumbnail:** extreme close-up — the white Nazca booby's wing with a small black finch's beak at
  the feather base and a **single bright bead of red blood** in the white down + 2–3 words
  **"IT DRINKS BLOOD"** or **"IT JUST LETS IT"** (yellow/white, thick black outline).

## The 6-beat outline
1. **Cold open (0:00–0:15):** one Nazca booby, right now, a small black finch at its wing, a bead of
   blood in the white feathers. Visceral macro. The jeopardy is the bleeding.
2. **Escalation (0:15–1:30):** one finch becomes six, all at the same wound. The booby swats — they
   hop off and come right back. It *could* leave. It doesn't. (curiosity deepens: **why won't it go?**)
3. **The turn (~1:30):** the reveal — this is an ordinary *seed-eating* finch. It has no business
   wanting blood. Double curiosity-gap: how did a finch learn to drink blood, and why does the seabird
   allow it? **[Pre-turn silence ~12s — FIX #1]**
4. **Payoff I — how it drinks (2:05–3:05):** the mechanics, slow — base of a growing flight feather,
   a beak built like a needle. **~30s HERO HOLD (FIX #2). Music peak here.**
5. **Deepen (3:05–5:15):** the bargain that soured — began as parasite-cleaning mutualism, became
   blood; and the barren islands that forced it (no water, dry season, blood as the last food).
6. **Climax — the trap (5:15–7:10):** the *real* reason it won't leave — the egg beneath it. The
   finches are egg-thieves; to rise is to lose the next generation. The wordless raid burst.
7. **Resolution (7:10–8:30):** the raid ends, finches resettle, the booby keeps its egg, one-line
   theme, departing wide over the endless sea.

**Runtime ~8:30 (510s). VO budget ≤765 words (~545 used → ~42% coverage, ~58% wordless — safe side).**

## Storyboard (scene by scene)
Legend — **Cut pace:** shots per scene & length. **Music:** one continuous bed, dynamic noted.
**SFX = diegetic ambience only, low in mix. NO whoosh/riser on any cut.**

### SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN · 0:00–0:15 · 4 shots (1.6/2.2/1.6/5s) — fast
- **On screen:** (1) OPEN ON a macro of the Nazca booby's white wing; a small black finch's beak jabs
  at the base of a flight feather. (2) **EXTREME MACRO:** a bead of bright red blood wells up in the
  white down; the finch's beak dips to it. (3) the booby turns its head, gapes its bill in a silent
  threat — but does not move. (4) wide-ish: the lone booby squats on a bare black-lava ledge, three
  little black finches around it, flat grey seabird-colony light, wind in the feathers.
- **VO:** *"Right now, on one of the loneliest islands on Earth, this giant seabird is being bled
  alive — by a bird small enough to close inside your hand."*
- **Music:** low sub drone, barely there.
- **SFX:** wind, distant colony calls, the tiny dry tap of the finch's beak.

### SCENE 2 — ESCALATION · 0:15–1:00 · 8 shots (~5s each) — settling
- **On screen:** more finches drop in — one at the wing, one at the base of the tail, one waiting on
  the lava. The booby swats sideways with its bill; a finch hops off and lands straight back. Macro:
  the pierced feather-base, red matting the white down. The booby shifts its weight but does not rise.
- **VO:** *"One finch became three. Now half a dozen take turns at the same raw wound on its wing. The
  seabird snaps at them — and they simply hop away, and come right back. It could stand. It could open
  those huge wings and be gone in a second. But it doesn't. It stays. And it bleeds."*
- **Music:** the bed enters — slow, minor, patient, quiet.
- **SFX:** wind, finch chatter, bill-snap, wingbeats. *(No cut hits.)*

### SCENE 3 — THE TOLL · 1:00–1:30 · 6 shots (~4–5s) — settling
- **On screen:** the wound open and weeping; a fly finds it; the booby's eye, tired, half-closed;
  dried blood streaked down the white flank; a slow push-in on the eye.
- **VO:** *"By now the wound is open and weeping, and others will find it too. And still the great bird
  holds its ground — as if being slowly emptied were somehow the safer choice."* **[PAUSE 3s]**
- **Music:** holds, adds a low note under the pause.
- **SFX:** wind, a fly, faint colony murmur.

### SCENE 4 — THE TURN · 1:30–2:05 · 4 shots (**~12s wordless hold**, then 5–6s) — FIX #1
- **On screen:** **[MUSIC-ONLY ~12s]** slow and near-still: the finch perched clean on the booby's
  back, a plain profile — a small black bird with a blunt, seed-cracking beak. Then the reveal cut:
  the *same* beak, its tip stained red.
- **VO:** *(after the wordless hold)* *"This is a ground finch. A seed-eater — the same kind of small,
  unremarkable bird Darwin watched cracking seeds here two centuries ago."* **[PAUSE 2s]** *"It has no
  business wanting blood at all. So how did a finch learn to drink it — and why does a seabird twenty
  times its size simply let it happen?"*
- **Music:** the first shift — a low, curious motif, restrained.
- **SFX:** wind, one dry seed-crack, a finch's thin call.

### SCENE 5 — PAYOFF I: HOW IT DRINKS · 2:05–3:05 · mix incl. a **~30s HERO HOLD** — money shot · FIX #2
- **On screen:** the mechanics, slow. The finch braces, selects the base of a *growing* flight
  feather — where the blood supply runs closest to the skin — and works its beak like a needle.
  **HERO HOLD (~30s, near-wordless):** one long, unbroken-feeling shot of the finch drinking at the
  wound, its throat working, the booby's eye half-closed and still. Then 3 quicker shots: a second
  finch at the tail base, blood on white down, the drinker lifting its red-tipped head.
- **VO (sparse over the hold):** *"It aims for the base of a new feather, where the blood runs closest
  to the surface, and opens it with a beak honed for exactly this."* **[HOLD — music carries ~14s]**
  *"Out here, close to a tenth of everything it eats is blood."*
- **Music:** **PEAK of the whole video here** — the theme opens up, dark and full. (First third ✓)
- **SFX:** tiny beak-taps, wind, one long low booby exhale.

### SCENE 6 — THE BARGAIN THAT SOURED · 3:05–4:05 · 12 shots (~4s) — body pace
- **On screen:** the origin, intercut clean-vs-bloodied: finches hopping over a booby's back picking
  off feather-mites and flies (the old mutualism); then the pivot — a beak breaks skin, a bead of
  blood, the finch pauses over it… and drinks. Back and forth between the helper and the parasite.
- **VO:** *"It did not begin as cruelty. Long ago, these finches earned their place on the boobies'
  backs — picking off the ticks and flies the big birds could never reach. A fair trade: the seabird
  got clean, the finch got fed. But every so often, a beak drew blood. And a bird starving on a bare
  rock does not forget the taste."*
- **Music:** eases down from the peak into a steady, contented-then-darkening middle.
- **SFX:** wind, finch chatter, low colony murmur.

### SCENE 7 — THE ISLAND THAT MADE IT · 4:05–5:15 · 14 shots (~5s) — body pace, some holds
- **On screen:** the barren truth of Wolf & Darwin — black lava, guano-white ledges, prickly pear,
  the sea on every side, no fresh water. Heat shimmer. A finch drinks nectar from a prickly-pear
  flower; pecks at seabird guano; then returns to the booby's wing. Sun-baked light sliding to dusk.
- **VO:** *"These are the two most remote islands in all the Galápagos. No streams. No soft ground.
  For half of every year the rain simply stops, and the seeds run out. So the finch drinks whatever
  it can find — flower nectar, even seabird droppings — and, when there is nothing else, the one thing
  that never runs dry on an island full of birds. Blood."* **[PAUSE 4s over a dusk shot]**
- **Music:** softer, wandering — a bleak kind of wonder.
- **SFX:** wind, distant surf, evening seabirds.

### SCENE 8 — THE SECOND TRAP BEGINS · 5:15–6:15 · 10 shots (5–6s, tightening)
- **On screen:** pull focus to what is *under* the booby — a single chalk-white egg between its feet.
  A finch's head cocks, one eye fixed on the egg. The booby presses down over it. Cut: booby (pinned)
  / finch (eyeing the egg) / the wing-wound still bleeding. The dilemma, made visible.
- **VO:** *"But there is a deeper reason the seabird will not rise. Beneath it, in the dust, is an egg.
  And these same finches are egg-thieves. The instant the booby lifts off to escape them —"*
  **[PAUSE 2s]** *"— they take the very thing it was protecting. To save the next generation, it has
  to sit still, and be bled."*
- **Music:** the warm bed curdles — a low tension drone, a single ticking pulse begins.
- **SFX:** wind drops, finch calls sharpen, a low booby growl.

### SCENE 9 — THE RAID · 6:15–7:10 · **THE WORDLESS BURST — 10 sub-2s cuts, ~40s** — climax
- **On screen (WORDLESS, ~40s, music+SFX only):** a finch feints at the egg; the booby lunges to
  defend it and, for one second, its wing lifts — instantly three finches are at the exposed skin,
  drinking. Rapid cuts: a beak stabbing the feather-base / blood on white / a finch rolling the egg an
  inch toward the ledge / the booby's bill stabbing down / feathers and dust / the egg rocking back
  under the belly / finches scattering and re-landing. The trap holds: the booby keeps the egg, loses
  more blood.
- **VO:** *none.* **[MUSIC + SFX ONLY — this is the payoff of the whole build]**
- **Music:** **peak intensity** — the ticking pulse snaps into a driving hit-bed, then cuts to
  near-silence as the raid breaks off.
- **SFX:** frantic finch calls, wingbeats, the dry scrape of the egg on lava, the booby's snapping bill.

### SCENE 10 — RESOLUTION · 7:10–8:30 · 8 shots (holds, ~8–14s) — slowing
- **On screen:** the raid is over. One by one the finches settle back onto the booby's back, unhurried,
  a few still dabbing at the wound. The booby resettles over its egg, eye half-closed, white flank
  streaked with dried blood, breathing slow. Dawn light on black lava. Final: a wide of the lone booby
  on its ledge, small black birds riding it, the sea going on forever.
- **VO:** *"By morning it is quiet again. The finches ride the bird they feed on, as if nothing had
  passed between them. The seabird keeps its egg. The finch keeps its life. Out here, at the far edge
  of the world, survival isn't kindness — and it isn't cruelty —"* **[PAUSE 4s]** *"— it's only the
  bargain the island allows. Paid, one drop of blood at a time."*
- **Music:** resolves — the dark theme returns, gentle, then fades under wind.
- **SFX:** calmer finch chatter, wind, surf, one last soft booby huff. *(No CTA in the video; subscribe
  lives in the description.)*

## Verified biology (sources — checked 2026-07-22)
- Vampire ground finch = *Geospiza septentrionalis*, a Darwin's finch; **endemic to Wolf & Darwin
  Islands**, Galápagos; IUCN **Vulnerable**, <1,000 mature individuals (2022).
- **Drinks blood** of **Nazca and blue-footed boobies** by pecking the **base of growing flight
  feathers** until a wound opens, then drinking; ~a tenth of its diet is booby blood.
- **Normally a seed/insect/nectar eater**; blood-feeding spikes in the **dry season (≈July–Dec)** when
  seeds fail; also drinks flower nectar and seabird guano.
- **Evolved from parasite-removal mutualism** (finches once cleaned ectoparasites off boobies) into
  parasitism — a taste for blood from open cleaning wounds.
- **Boobies swat but tolerate it** because (a) several finches feed at once so defence is impractical,
  and (b) **the finches predate the boobies' eggs**, so a booby is **reluctant to leave the nest** —
  the "second trap" of Scene 8.

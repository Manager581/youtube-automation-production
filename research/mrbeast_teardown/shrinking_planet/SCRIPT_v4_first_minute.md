# SCRIPT v4 — first minute (0:00–1:00)

Supersedes SCRIPT_v3_first_minute.md. Built 2026-09-09 from four independently drafted variants
(two flash-forward opens, two live opens) judged by ear and against the measured reference hook,
then merged: the lean draft's beat grid and speech budget, the rich draft's contestant exchanges.

Why the FLASH-FORWARD open and not the live open: `lane.promise.proof_shots` is `["S000"]` and
`deliver.py` requires WATCH_NOTES `first_3s` to name that shot. Both live-open drafts either
deleted S000 or asserted the promise in prose without naming it, which the story gate now refuses.
Choosing a live open is therefore an owner-level change to `lane.promise`, not a script edit.

Beat grid matches the manifest for B02–B06 exactly; B01's five shots (S001–S005) compress into
0:03–0:12 to make room for the 3-second cold open.

- Reuse takes, verbatim, no new voice spend: "Helmets off." / "Three years ago." / "Still sore."
  / "Running." / "Both locked." / "Have fun down there."
- Every ORB line declares (on) or (vo). ORB is (vo) only in B00 and B06, the two beats whose
  shots hold no ORB — B00 is S000 (PIP + GRUFF on the rock), B06 is S022/S023.
- PIP has exactly one mouth:ON line, on S010, the one talking clip Law 2(b) permits in the minute.

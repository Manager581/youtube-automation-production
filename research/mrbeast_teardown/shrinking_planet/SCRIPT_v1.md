# SHRINKING PLANET — Production Script v1

Runtime 20:00. All-AI episode. Cast: ORB (host, chrome sphere, no mouth — speech = halo pulse), PIP (magenta axolotl, small mouth, yellow scarf), GRUFF (cyan yeti, fur-covered mouth, cracked brass goggle on forehead), THE COUNT (black cube scoreboard, beeps only).

Conventions: ORB visits at midnight; the story day rolls over on THE COUNT the moment the planet changes size. THE RED LINE around ORB's landing pad = the QUIT device (cross it, both go home, no prize). THE LAUNCH KEY (Day 25) = the STEAL device (turn it, one seat, one winner). They are never the same object.

PIP mouth:ON = her mouth is on camera while she speaks (cap 20). mouth:OFF = voice-over on a reaction, insert, or a shot of what she is looking at.

**ORB** (vo, energy: soft, closing the act): That's a truce. It will last exactly until I come back — which, for the record, is in three days.

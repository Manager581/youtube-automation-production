# SHRINKING PLANET — SCRIPT v2

Source of truth: `research/mrbeast_teardown/SHRINKING_PLANET_bible_v2.html` (v2 outline). v2 = v1 + the adversarial review's landed fixes (clock ladder re-valued, offers moved to the evening BEFORE each shrink so every beat carries one planet size, prize pot stated once, five same-beat paraphrases rewritten). Runtime 20:00, 36 beats (the bible's 30 beats, with five long beats split so no stretch runs more than 60 s without a beat header). Cast: ORB (host, halo-pulse speech), PIP (magenta, mouth-visible lines ≤20), GRUFF (cyan, fur-covered mouth), THE COUNT (beeps only). All dialogue original. PIP `mouth:ON` = her mouth is on camera while she speaks (lip-sync shot); `mouth:OFF` = voice-over on a reaction or insert.

Ladder (day → planet metres, THE COUNT always matches): 1→1000 · 5→500 · 10→300 · 20→25 · 25→20 · 26→12 · 27→7 · 28→4 · 29→2 · 30→2. Every shrink lands at the START of a beat (B10 Day 5 sunrise, B14 Day 10, B22 Day 20 sunrise); the offers that cause them are made the evening before (B09 = Day 4, B21 = Day 19), so no beat straddles two planet sizes.

## B06 [00:45-01:30] Planet tour
[DAY 1]
[PLANET 1000]

Orbital push-in onto the north pole.

**ORB** (vo, energy: tour-guide): One thousand metres across. Its own sky, if you squint. Its own sunrise every eleven hours, because I got the spin slightly wrong and I'm not fixing it now. Her camp is at the north pole.

PIP lays three ration bars in a row and looks at the horizon.

**PIP** (mouth:OFF, emotion: bright): It's actually kind of nice? The horizon's right there. You could walk to the edge of the world before breakfast.

**ORB** (vo, energy: tour-guide): His cave is at the south. On a planet this size they can go a week without seeing each other. Which, from what I can tell, is the plan.

GRUFF thumps down on the ledge inside the cave. THE COUNT drifts past, faces blank, then lit: DAY 1 · 1,000 M.

**GRUFF** (on, emotion: flat): It's a hole. I like a hole. Nobody sings in a hole.

**ORB** (vo, energy: dry): He's brought one mattress, one paperback, and a rock he uses as a second rock. That's the whole inventory. She brought a keyboard. Neither of them has mentioned the other's luggage.

**ORB** (vo, energy: tour-guide): That's THE COUNT. It keeps the day and it keeps the size. The size is the only thing on this planet that changes — and it only ever goes one way.

The toilet: a hole in the rock with a view of the stars. GRUFF looks down, looks up, looks at the camera, and sits.

**ORB** (vo, energy: dry): Facilities.

The crate. A slow arc around it; the question-mark plate catches the light.

**ORB** (vo, energy: tour-guide): And on the equator: the crate. Sealed. Nobody opens it before Day 20. Not them. Not me. It has a lock and I do not have hands.

**PIP** (mouth:OFF, emotion: curious): You don't know what's in it?

**ORB** (vo, energy: dry): I know exactly what's in it. That's the whole point of me.

The two of them walk the equator, three metres of slack tether between them, neither looking at the other.

## B08 [02:00-02:45] The engine
[DAY 2]
[PLANET 1000]
[COUNT 1000]
[MUSIC bed]
[TEXT "DAY 2"]
[TEXT "1,000 M → 2 M"]
[SFX ladder graphic ticks as each rung draws]

Two sunrises, split-screen: her tent, his cave, opposite hemispheres. PIP stretches into hard low light.

**ORB** (vo, energy: explaining): Day two. Here's the engine. Every deal I offer them shrinks this planet. Not a fine, not a penalty, not a score. Rock, gone, forever. They can say no to any deal. Most people don't, because the deals are very good and the rock is very boring.

The planet rotates; a diameter ring draws around it.

**ORB** (vo, energy: explaining): Right now they can avoid each other. At three hundred metres they'll share a sunrise whether they like it or not. At a hundred metres they'll hear each other breathe. At two, there's nowhere to stand but together.

A ladder draws itself: 1,000 → 500 → 300 → 25 → 2.

**ORB** (on, energy: quiet): So. Does a smaller world bring them back — or finish them off? I genuinely don't know. That's why I built it.

Extreme wide: two tiny figures on opposite sides of a ridge, facing away. The question lands on silence.

**GRUFF** (on, emotion: flat): Two metres. He said two metres.

**PIP** (mouth:OFF, emotion: small): I heard him.

**ORB** (vo, energy: explaining): For the record, nobody has ever reached two metres on one of my planets. The furthest anyone got was nineteen, and they crossed the ring on Day 22 holding hands, which I still think about.

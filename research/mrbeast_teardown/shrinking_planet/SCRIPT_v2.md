# SHRINKING PLANET — SCRIPT v2

Source of truth: `research/mrbeast_teardown/SHRINKING_PLANET_bible_v2.html` (v2 outline). v2 = v1 + the adversarial review's landed fixes (clock ladder re-valued, offers moved to the evening BEFORE each shrink so every beat carries one planet size, prize pot stated once, five same-beat paraphrases rewritten). Runtime 20:00, 36 beats (the bible's 30 beats, with five long beats split so no stretch runs more than 60 s without a beat header). Cast: ORB (host, halo-pulse speech), PIP (magenta, mouth-visible lines ≤20), GRUFF (cyan, fur-covered mouth), THE COUNT (beeps only). All dialogue original. PIP `mouth:ON` = her mouth is on camera while she speaks (lip-sync shot); `mouth:OFF` = voice-over on a reaction or insert.

Ladder (day → planet metres, THE COUNT always matches): 1→1000 · 5→500 · 10→300 · 20→25 · 25→20 · 26→12 · 27→7 · 28→4 · 29→2 · 30→2. Every shrink lands at the START of a beat (B10 Day 5 sunrise, B14 Day 10, B22 Day 20 sunrise); the offers that cause them are made the evening before (B09 = Day 4, B21 = Day 19), so no beat straddles two planet sizes.

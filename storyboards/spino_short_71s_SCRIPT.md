# SPINOSAURUS — 71-second wordless Short — SECOND BY SECOND

**Title:** `This Dinosaur Was Built to Sink`
**Runtime:** 71.0 s (66.5 s picture + 4.5 s silent end card) — matches the reference exactly.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 720×1280, 24 fps. **NO narration. NO captions. NO music** (one low drone only, Act 2).
**Shots:** 20 + card. Mean 3.3 s. **Longest 4.6 s** — every shot fits inside ONE 6 s Grok generation.

**Why this title:** it is an expectation violation *and* it is true. Spinosaurus had unusually
dense, solid limb bones (Fabbri et al. 2022) — ballast for staying under, where other dinosaurs
would float. The video's whole job is to make you watch that sentence happen.

**The rule the film teaches (per the reference's structure):** *what you read as riverbank is alive.*
Taught at 0:04 as a delight, repeated at 1:03 as the closing rhyme.

**Creature lock:** ONE approved Spinosaurus seed image drives every shot as i2v. Sail = tall,
red-veined membrane with a notch. Snout = long, narrow, crocodile-like, conical teeth. Do not
let the design drift between shots.

## ⚠️ REALISM IS THE WHOLE SPEC — read this before anything else

**Target: it must look like real footage a real crew shot.** Not stylised, not graded,
not "cinematic." Real.

**NO IMPOSED GRADE.** My previous attempt failed because I read the reference's measured
saturation numbers (12–28% swamp / 48–81% underwater / 19–33% rock) as a *look* and applied a
filter to force them. They are not a look. They are **three real environments photographed
honestly** — an overcast mud swamp genuinely is desaturated khaki; clear water genuinely is
saturated cyan; wet black rock under cloud genuinely is grey-green. The arc emerges from the
locations, not from a LUT.

So:
- **Each location gets its own true, natural palette.** Dawn river = warm dust, green reeds,
  brown water, pale sky. Underwater = real green-gold river light, visibility falling off with
  depth. Surface = bright, full contrast. **Do not push them toward each other.**
- **Grade only for continuity WITHIN one location** — matching exposure and white balance
  between clips of the same place. Nothing more. No saturation pushes, no gamma crushing,
  no colour-channel mixing.
- **Keep full tonal range.** Real footage has true blacks AND clean highlights. The last
  attempt had neither — everything sat in a grey-green middle.

**REAL SOUND.** Turn Grok's audio ON and **keep the native per-clip audio** — that is real
diegetic sound tied to the picture, and it is exactly what the reference does (Lynx keeps
Veo's clip audio rather than replacing it). Do NOT lay a synthetic drone over the top; that is
what makes it read as a slideshow with a mood track. Layer additional SFX only where a beat
needs weight (the reveal, the strike, the breach), and mix them UNDER the native audio.

**REAL CAMERA.** Every prompt must specify real optics and real operating: a physical camera,
natural available light, believable depth of field, slight handheld weight where a human
operator would have it, locked off where a tripod would be. No drifting "AI camera," no
impossible moves, no lens flares, no volumetric glow effects.

**Cut-hiding, correctly stated:** cuts hide when consecutive shots share **the same real
place, the same time of day and the same light direction** — because that is what real
coverage looks like. It is continuity, not a colour filter. Shoot each act as one location.
